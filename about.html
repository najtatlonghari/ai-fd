About ScamShield

Helping people stay safer online.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help people recognise suspicious emails and messages and make safer decisions online.

Why Email Safety Matters

Scam emails can look as though they come from trusted companies, banks or people. They may try to trick people into clicking unsafe links, sharing private information or sending money.

Our Goal

We want to make online safety easier to understand by sharing useful information about common scams and the warning signs people should look for.

Stay safe: Always check unexpected messages carefully before clicking links or sharing personal information.
